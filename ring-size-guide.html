Two Ways to Measure at Home

  1. String or paper method: wrap a strip of paper or a piece of string snugly around the base of your finger, mark where it overlaps, then measure that length against a ruler — that's your circumference in mm.
  2. Existing ring method: measure the inside diameter of a ring that already fits well, in millimetres, and use the "Diameter" toggle above instead.
