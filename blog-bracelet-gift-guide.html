The Handmade Bracelet Gift Guide

A quick matchmaking guide from birthdays to "just because" — because the right cord says more than the card.

For a Birthday

Birthdays are the easiest gifting occasion because there's no need to guess sentimentally — a colour they already wear a lot of is the safest bet. A two-tone twisted cord bracelet in their favourite shade reads as thoughtful without needing to know their exact wrist size, since the adjustable knot does the sizing for you.

For an Anniversary

Anniversaries lean toward something with a bit more presence — a chunky woven bangle or a toggle-clasp piece feels more considered than a slim everyday cord, without needing to spend on precious metal. If you know their wrist size already (or can measure a bracelet they already own), it's worth choosing a closer, more tailored fit rather than the most adjustable option.

For a Best Friend or Sister

This is where slim, stackable cord bracelets earn their keep — they're easy to layer with jewellery the recipient already owns, inexpensive enough to gift without a special occasion attached, and forgiving on fit thanks to the sliding knot. A lot of our customers buy two of the same style, one for themselves and one to give away.

For Yourself (No Occasion Required)

Not every purchase needs a reason. If you're buying for yourself, this is the one time we'd actually recommend measuring your wrist properly first — run it through our bracelet size guide so the fit is exactly right from the first day, rather than adjusting the sliding knot in over the following week.

Whichever occasion you're shopping for, every style mentioned here — and the rest of the range — is browsable on our collection page, with prices and materials listed on each piece.
