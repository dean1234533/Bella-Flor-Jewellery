Bracelet & Wrist Size Guide

Measure once, order with confidence.

Measure your wrist, choose how you like a bracelet to sit, and we'll recommend a bracelet length. Our cord bracelets use an adjustable sliding knot, so this gives you a comfortable starting range rather than one fixed number.

Bracelet Size Calculator

How to Measure Your Wrist

  1. Wrap a soft tape measure around your wrist just below the wrist bone, where you'd naturally wear a bracelet.
  2. No tape measure? A strip of paper or a shoelace works — mark the overlap point and measure it against a ruler.
  3. Keep it snug but not tight, then enter the number above along with your preferred fit.
