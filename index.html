Talk to us ↗
Do you deliver across the UK?

Yes — every order ships free across the UK. See our Terms & Conditions for estimated delivery times.

Can I order a custom or bespoke design?

Yes, custom colour combinations and metallic accents are available. Get in touch via WhatsApp or our contact page to discuss your design.

How do I know what size to order?

Most of our bracelets use an adjustable sliding knot, so one size fits a wide range of wrists. If you'd like an exact fit, try our free bracelet size guide.

How do I look after my bracelet?

Keep it away from water, perfume and lotion where possible, and store it flat between wears. Our full care guide covers everything.

What if my bracelet doesn't fit?

The adjustable sliding knot on most styles can be loosened or tightened after delivery. If you're still not happy, contact us and we'll help sort it out.
